## Access is a starting point

Access to technology can open a door. Practical knowledge, time to learn, and a community of support help people move through it.

## Questions worth asking

- Who has the resources to experiment?
- Which barriers are local and practical?
- How can learning translate into meaningful opportunity?

> A useful initiative starts by listening to the people it hopes to serve.
