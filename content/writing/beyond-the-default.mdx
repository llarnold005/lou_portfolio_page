## A default is a decision

Technical defaults make systems easier to use. They also carry assumptions about whose needs matter and which tradeoffs are acceptable.

## Make room for a better question

Before changing a system, it helps to understand why it behaves the way it does. Independent thinking starts with curiosity and evidence, rather than disagreement for its own sake.

A digital notebook can hold these unfinished questions while longer essays give them room to develop.
